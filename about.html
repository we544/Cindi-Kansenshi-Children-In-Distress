Who We Are

CINDI Kansenshi is a community-based organisation in Nkwazi compound, Ndola, working with orphaned and vulnerable children and their families.

Our Story

Born from the Community

CINDI Kansenshi stands for Children in Distress. It was founded in 1994 by four ladies concerned for the plight of orphans and vulnerable children in Nkwazi. They used to meet under a tree in the community and work out how to help the children who approached them.

It was at the start of the AIDS pandemic, and many children were caring for ill and dying parents, with no money for food or school fees. With the advent of ARVs, the HIV story has changed, but poverty, suffering, and need remain. The challenges within our communities are still much the same 32 years later.

Today, CINDI works with children in their extended or adopted families and communities. Alongside schooling, nutrition, health education, clothing, and medical support, the organisation is increasingly helping young people reach skills training and third-level education.

Our Mission

To assist and support children within their extended or adopted families, in their own communities, so they can grow up healthy, happy, and self-supporting members of society.

Our Vision

A future where children in Nkwazi have access to education, nutrition, health support, and the tools to become resilient leaders in their own communities.

Our Values

  • Dignity — every child is treated with respect
  • Transparency — open books, honest reporting
  • Co-operation — families contribute to their community, not charity alone
  • Compassion — we lead with love
Our People

Meet the Team

Board of Volunteers

Leadership

The structure of CINDI is guided by a board of volunteers committed to community care and accountability.

Programme Team

Implementation

Our team manages education, nutrition, family support, and community development work across Nkwazi.

Centre Staff

Operations

We currently have 5 members of staff at the Centre, including a coordinator, two project managers, a field worker, and a security guard.

Caregivers & Families

Community

Families contribute to a duty rota to keep the centre and its surroundings clean. CINDI values this shared responsibility as co-operation that preserves dignity.

Working Together

Support With Dignity

Families as partners

CINDI families are encouraged to contribute in practical ways, including taking turns to care for the centre and its surroundings. Support is paired with shared responsibility.

Growing livelihoods

Families cultivate food on the CINDI farm. The participating guardians have formed a co-operative to work together and strengthen their livelihoods.

Education beyond school

CINDI is expanding its focus on skills training and third-level education, helping students with needs such as accommodation, food, devices, and peer mentoring.
