Who We Are

A community-rooted organisation fighting for children's right to education, nutrition, and a dignified childhood in Ndola, Zambia.

Our Story

Born from the Community

CINDI Kansenshi was founded by a small group of neighbours who refused to look away. In the Kansenshi compound, HIV/AIDS had left hundreds of children without parents, without school fees, and without consistent meals.

What started as a weekly feeding programme under a mango tree grew into a registered organisation reaching over 1,200 children and their caregivers each year. Every decision we make is guided by the community we serve.

We work not for the community — but with it.

Our Mission

To reduce vulnerability of children and their caregivers through education, nutrition, health services, and household empowerment in the Kansenshi area.

Our Vision

A Zambia where every child has access to quality education, proper nutrition, and the opportunity to reach their full potential in a safe, loving environment.

Our Values

  • Dignity — every child is treated with respect
  • Transparency — open books, honest reporting
  • Community — decisions made with locals, not for them
  • Compassion — we lead with love
