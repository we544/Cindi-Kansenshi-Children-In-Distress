Education Support

No Child Left Out of Class

We believe education is the single greatest equaliser. CINDI covers school fees, uniforms, textbooks, and stationery for every enrolled child — removing the barriers that keep vulnerable learners at home.

Our after-school tutoring centre opens Monday to Friday from 2 pm – 5 pm, staffed by qualified teachers and trained volunteers. Children who are behind grade level receive targeted one-on-one catch-up sessions.

Nutrition & Feeding

A Full Stomach Changes Everything

Hunger is the most immediate barrier to learning. Our on-site feeding programme provides a hot, balanced meal every school day to children who would otherwise attend on an empty stomach.

Beyond daily meals, our home food packages — distributed monthly — support the most vulnerable households, ensuring caregivers can feed children through the school-break periods too.

Health & Wellness

Healthy Children, Brighter Futures

Our mobile health clinic — run in partnership with Ndola Teaching Hospital — visits the compound quarterly to provide free check-ups, immunisations, deworming, and basic dental screening.

We also run HIV/AIDS awareness sessions and connect caregivers to antiretroviral treatment support. Our trained social workers provide ongoing psychosocial support to children who have experienced trauma or loss.

Quarterly mobile health clinics
HIV counselling & ART linkage
Psychosocial support (social workers)
Immunisation & deworming campaigns
Partner on Health
Community Empowerment

Sustainable Change Starts with Caregivers

Children thrive when their households are stable. Our caregiver empowerment programme trains guardians — many of them elderly grandmothers — in income-generating skills such as soap-making, tailoring, and small-business management.

We also facilitate Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs), giving caregivers access to credit and a financial safety net for the first time.

Small-business mentorship
