Come Visit Us

Our doors are open. Come see the work, meet the children, and experience the heart of Kansenshi firsthand.

Find Us in Kansenshi, Ndola

CINDI Kansenshi is located just past Nkwazi Community School in the Kansenshi compound, Ndola. Look for our green-painted gate with the CINDI sign — you can't miss it.

Getting Here

From Ndola CBD: Take the Kabushi Road toward Kansenshi. Turn at Nkwazi Community School — CINDI is immediately after the school on the left.

By Minibus

Take any Kansenshi-bound minibus from the Ndola Town Centre bus station. Ask the conductor to drop you at Nkwazi School.

Plan Your Visit

What to Expect When You Arrive

01

Reception & Welcome

You'll be greeted at the gate by our Community Liaison Officer, Daniel, who will introduce you to the centre and brief you on our current programmes.

02

Tour of the Centre

Walk through our classrooms, feeding hall, tutoring room, and community garden. See our impact firsthand and ask questions along the way.

03

Meet the Children

If visiting during programme hours, you may have the opportunity to interact with the children during lunch or a tutoring session (subject to safeguarding policies).

04

Meet the Team

Our staff will walk you through impact reports, annual accounts, and our future expansion plans — full transparency guaranteed.

Child Safeguarding Policy

All visitors are required to sign in and are accompanied by a staff member at all times. Photography of children requires prior written consent from CINDI management. We take the safety and dignity of our children seriously.

Schools & Organisations

Plan a Group Visit

We welcome delegations from schools, churches, corporates, embassies, and NGOs. Group visits can be arranged Monday to Friday and include a full centre tour, a programme briefing, and the opportunity to interact with staff.

For groups of 10 or more, please give us at least 5 working days' notice so we can prepare properly.
