Our Journey

From a Tree to a Community Response

1994

Founded by Concerned Women

Four local women begin meeting under a tree in Nkwazi to care for orphaned and vulnerable children who were going hungry and missing school.

Early years

Food, care, and practical support

CINDI responds to the crisis of AIDS, poverty, and child-headed households by helping children access food, schooling, and stable community care.

Today

Community School and Family Support

Our school serves around 400 pupils in grades 1–7, with 63 secondary learners supported, plus food packages and farm-based livelihoods for families.
